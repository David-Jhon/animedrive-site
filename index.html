Level 03 · Quest log

Questions, answered.

よくある質問と実績。

Q1Is AnimeDrive free?

Yes. Every command is free to use, and there's no premium tier.

Q2Why does the bot ask me to join channels?

To use AnimeDrive you need to join two channels first. The bot shows a Join button for each one. Join both, tap Try Again, and you're in.

Q3Do I need to sign up?

No. Your Telegram account is all you need. Open @animedrive_bot and send /start.

Q4What does the bot store about me?

What Telegram shares with every bot: your Telegram ID, name and username. It also keeps when you joined, when you were last active, and which commands you use. If you link AniList, it saves your AniList username, and notes you save with /note are kept so you can read them later.

Q5Something failed or the bot didn't answer.

Wait a minute and try again, or pick another option from the menu. If it keeps happening, tell us with /request.

Q6Where's the full command list?

Send /help in the bot, or browse the command deck above.

Q7Do the game orbs change anything in the bot?

No, the game is just for fun. Every command works from the start. Your game progress and achievements are saved in this browser only.
